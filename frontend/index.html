FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. CyberMind AI is a local platform, so the analysis happens on your own machine.

Do I need security knowledge to use it?

No. Each result includes a risk score and a plain-language explanation of what was found.

Can it guarantee something is safe?

No tool can. A low score means fewer warning signs were found, so keep using good judgment with unknown senders and files.

What can I analyze?

URLs, messages, supported files, image metadata and security events such as suspicious logins.

What should I do if a result says High risk?

Don't open the link or file, and don't enter any passwords. Delete the message, and if you already interacted with it, change the affected password and tell your IT contact.

Do I need an account?

Yes. An account opens your dashboard, where your scans and security events are kept together.
